Welcome to my personal site!

This space was born to serve as a professional presentation, but above all as an **open technical notebook and collection of thoughts**. Here I'll share lessons learned, software experiments, ongoing projects, and insights into software engineering.

## Why a personal website?

In a landscape full of walled gardens and fast-paced social feeds, having your own corner on the open web allows you to:

1. **Own your content**: Write in Markdown, version-controlled with Git, free from algorithmic feeds.
2. **Document the learning curve**: As engineers, knowledge that isn't documented often fades away. Writing articles solidifies deep understanding.
3. **Engage with peers**: Through the comment section below each post, we can discuss architectural approaches and exchange ideas.

> "The best time to plant a tree was 20 years ago. The second best time is now."

## Tech stack powering this site

I chose **Astro** paired with **Tailwind CSS** for this project. The core highlights include:

- **Blazing speed**: Zero unnecessary JavaScript shipped by default.
- **Native bilingual support**: Smooth switching between English and Spanish.
- **Giscus comments**: Seamless integration backed by GitHub Discussions, privacy-friendly and tracker-free.
