Design and development of a personal platform for sharing technical projects, professional journey, and blog publications.

### Key Highlights:
- **AcademicPages-inspired layout**: Sticky profile sidebar with contact details, bio, and social links.
- **Full Internationalization (i18n)**: Seamless support for English and Spanish with an instant switcher.
- **Dark / Light Theme**: System preference detection with smooth manual toggle and zero FOUC.
- **GitHub Discussions Comments**: Giscus integration for transparent community engagement.
- **Automated CI/CD Deployment**: GitHub Actions pipeline for publishing to `jviera.github.io`.
